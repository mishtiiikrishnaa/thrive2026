the room where it decides

every choice a model makes happens in a space you can't see. here, you can.

a thought enters the room hungry for form. it passes first through pattern, where shape is pulled from noise and what doesn't fit is already thinning out. it passes through weight, where some paths are honoured and most are quietly dismissed. it passes through connection, where what survives is bound to everything else, until only one decision is left standing — and that, in the dark, small and lit, is the choice. it looks obvious from the outside. that's the trick of it.
