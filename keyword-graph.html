training

training

an ai learns from staggering amounts of text — books, code, conversations — and finds statistical patterns within them. no rules are written by hand.

weights

weights

millions of small numbers nudged during training. the model stores its memory as these weights, adjusted slowly by error.

attention

attention

transformers don't read linearly. attention weighs every token against every other to decide what matters for the next prediction.

inference

inference

prompt runs the maths forward: predict next token, repeat. an answer emerges from pure probability, not understanding.

backprop

backprop

error measured, pushed backward through every layer. weights shift slightly. repeated billions of times, mistakes shrink.

generation

generation

nothing truly created. the model joins probable pieces of what it has seen into something that feels original.

tokenization

tokenization

text chopped into pieces — tokens — mapped to numbers. the model never sees words, only arithmetic on ids.

creativity

creativity

temperature controls randomness. high: vivid, risky, sometimes wrong. low: safe, dull. imagination is a dial.

hallucination

hallucination

confidently invents false facts. predicts what sounds right, not true. grounding in real facts fixes this.

embedding

embedding

each token a high-dimensional vector. similar meanings live near each other. arithmetic works on meaning.

gradient

gradient

backprop sends corrections backward. in deep networks signals vanish or explode. careful plumbing keeps learning alive.

temperature

temperature

a randomness dial. low = safe. high = wild. creativity and accuracy trade off directly.
