obsidian knowledge graph

training

🍞

an ai learns from staggering amounts of text — books, code, conversations — and finds statistical patterns within them. no rules are written by hand.

weights

🧠

millions of small numbers nudged during training. the model stores its memory as these weights, adjusted slowly by error.

attention

🌐

transformers don't read linearly. attention weighs every token against every other to decide what matters for the next prediction.

inference

🪄

prompt runs the maths forward: predict next token, repeat. an answer emerges from pure probability, not understanding.

backprop

📉

error is measured, then pushed backward through every layer. weights shift slightly. repeated billions of times, mistakes shrink.

generation

🔁

nothing is truly created. the model joins probable pieces of what it has seen into something that feels original.

tokenization

🔤

text is chopped into pieces — tokens — then mapped to numbers. the model never sees words, only arithmetic on ids.

creativity

🎲

temperature controls randomness. high: vivid, risky, sometimes wrong. low: safe, dull, predictable. imagination is a dial.

hallucination

👻

the model confidently invents false facts. it predicts what sounds right, not what is true. grounding in real facts fixes this.

embedding

🧮

each token becomes a high-dimensional vector. similar meanings live near each other. arithmetic works on meaning.

gradient

🌊

backprop sends corrections backward. in deep networks, signals can vanish or explode. careful plumbing keeps learning alive.

temperature

🎲

a randomness dial for the next token. low = safe. high = wild. creativity and accuracy trade off directly.
